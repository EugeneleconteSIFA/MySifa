Environment and CSR

Reduce your waste with linerless.

Reducing our footprint has been one of our values from the start. Linerless technology is its most direct expression: in some applications, it removes the glassine liner from your labels.

Conventional label

Silicone and glassine thrown away

On application, the label is peeled off its liner. The silicone-coated liner is no longer needed and becomes waste.

Linerless SIFA

No more glassine

The silicone layer moves to the printed side: wound on a roll, each label rests on the one before it without a glassine liner. No more glassine to throw away or recycle.

Because we do our own coating, the adhesive coat weight is adjusted to your application.

The company

Nearly 40 years in Hauts-de-France.

Founded in 1987, SIFA is a French company specialising in the manufacture of self-adhesive labels. Our 3,000 m² site near Lille is equipped to provide made-to-measure and personalised labelling solutions.

Based since 2018 in a modern factory in Roubaix, we produce continuously: a dedicated production team working two or three 8-hour shifts, 5 to 6 days a week. A second site is planned for 2027 as part of our business continuity plan.
