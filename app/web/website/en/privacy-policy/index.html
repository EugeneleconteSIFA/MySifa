Privacy policy

The data SIFA receives through this website, what it does with it and how to exercise your rights.

This English version is provided for convenience. In case of discrepancy, the French version prevails.

Data controller

SIFA, SAS, 45 rue Rollin, 59100 Roubaix, France. SIREN 340 885 003. Contact: contact@sifa.pro, +33 (0)3 20 69 01 01.

What the website collects

The website has no user accounts, no audience measurement tools and no advertising cookies.

The quote request form does not send anything to our servers: it prepares an e-mail in your mail application, which you review and then send yourself to contact@sifa.pro. The data we receive is therefore what you choose to tell us, by e-mail or by telephone:

  • identity and contact details: name, company, e-mail address, telephone number;
  • description of your requirements: product, quantity, use, equipment.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Responding to your request and preparing a quotation: pre-contractual measures taken at your request.
  • Managing the business relationship with our professional customers and prospects: SIFA's legitimate interest.
  • Complying with our accounting and tax obligations when the request results in an order: legal obligation.

Retention period

  • Request not followed up: 3 years from your last contact.
  • Customer: for the duration of the business relationship, then 3 years after it ends.
  • Accounting records (accepted quotations, invoices): 10 years, the statutory period.

Recipients

Your data is read by the SIFA teams responsible for quotations, sales administration and production. It is hosted by our technical service providers (e-mail, website hosting), who act on our instructions. It is not sold, rented or transferred to third parties.

Third-party services loaded by the website

The website's fonts are provided by Google Fonts. To display them, your browser contacts Google's servers, which receive your IP address. This processing is governed by Google's privacy policy.

The website's host, Hostinger, keeps technical logs (IP address, page requested, date) to ensure the security and proper operation of the server.

Cookies and local storage

The website does not set any cookies. It only stores, in your browser's local storage, the display theme you have chosen (light or dark). This information stays on your device, is never sent to us and can be deleted from your browser settings. It does not require consent.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability of your data, as well as the right to object to processing based on our legitimate interest. To exercise these rights, write to contact@sifa.pro or to SIFA, 45 rue Rollin, 59100 Roubaix, France. We respond within one month.

If you believe that your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (French data protection authority): www.cnil.fr.
