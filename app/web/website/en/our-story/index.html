Our story

SIFA, self-adhesive label stock coater in Roubaix since 1987

Founded in 1987, SIFA is a French company specialising in the manufacture of self-adhesive labels. With almost 40 years of experience as an adhesive coater, it controls the entire manufacturing process, from producing the label stock through to delivery.
