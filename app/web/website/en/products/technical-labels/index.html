Technical labels: special adhesives and heavyweight stocks

Cold, deep-freeze, tyres, labels that must come off: when a standard label will not hold, we build the adhesive laminate. As a coater, SIFA selects the hot-melt adhesive, sets its coat weight and adds adhesive-free zones where needed.

Expertise

What coating makes possible

Adjustable coat weight

More adhesive where the application requires it: tyres, linerless, food processing in damp environments.

Adhesive-free zones

Areas left without adhesive at the label edge prevent adhesive ooze and build-up on machines.

Face stocks for cold conditions

Thermal papers in deep-freeze grades, matt white PP from 80 to 120 µm.

Heavyweight stocks

Over-labelling labels, for example in 250 g matt coated paper, and heavyweight linerless.
