Printing

From one colour to four-colour process

Printed labels, promotional labels, product labels: printing is done on the same line as coating and die-cutting.

1 to 2 colours

On our 2-colour rotary press.

Multi-colour and four-colour process

On our 3-colour rotary press: four-colour process is done in 2 passes.

Varnish and reverse printing

Protective varnish, front printing and spot reverse printing available.

Flexographic printing, UV inks

Photopolymer plates made for each design.
