Linerless labels

A linerless label has no glassine liner: the silicone layer is applied to the printed face, and each label rests on the one before it in the roll. In automatic application, there is no liner left to rewind or throw away.

Request a quote All product families
What changes

Less material, less waste

On a conventional label, the siliconised liner only serves to carry the label to the point of application. See the comparison on the home page.

No liner to throw away

In some applications, linerless technology eliminates the glassine liner: no more glassine to dispose of or reprocess.

Adjusted adhesive coat weight

As a coater, SIFA applies the adhesive itself and sets the coat weight to suit your application.

Over-labelling

Over-labelling labels in heavyweight linerless stock.

Made in Roubaix

Adhesive laminate, printing and packaging, all carried out on our own site.
