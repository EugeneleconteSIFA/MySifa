Continuous feed

For printers that draw the web continuously, with no roll or core.

Available options

Fanfold options
Face stockVellum paper (matt, white, tinted, fluorescent yellow or orange), gloss or matt coated paper, thermal papers (eco thermal, top-coated thermal, deep-freeze versions), black opaque vellum; matt white PP film, one or two sides, from 80 to 120 µm.
AdhesivePermanent hot-melt, high-tack permanent, removable, repositionable, deep-freeze, chilled, tyre labels; permanent/permanent double coating available. Adhesive coat weight matched to the application, adhesive-free zones on request.
PrintingBlank, 1 to 2 colours, multi-colour or four-colour process with protective varnish; front and spot reverse printing available. Flexographic printing, UV inks, photopolymer plates made for each design.
FormatRectangular or round (common diameters from 30 to 50 mm), perforation between labels available, white underprint.
